What does CTSG do?+

CTSG (Calamity Technology Solutions Group) helps home-service companies book more jobs from customers they already have. I connect your field software and QuickBooks into one customer database you own, find the follow-ups worth making (unsold estimates, overdue customers and maintenance plan candidates), run the campaigns you approve, and report the work they bring back.

Who is CTSG for?+

Home-service companies in Harris and Fort Bend counties doing $1M to $10M a year with customer history to work from: HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing, pest control, pool and landscaping companies.

What software do you work with?+

I start with the systems you already use, such as Housecall Pro, Jobber and QuickBooks. Exports from your field software and accounting feed one database.

How much does it cost?+

A one-time setup of $2,500 to $3,500 brings your records together into your own database; the fee depends on how many systems you run. Ongoing analysis, approved campaigns and the results report are $1,500 to $2,500 a month, month to month after the first three months.

How long does setup take?+

Connecting your customer history usually takes 2 to 3 weeks. After that, the analysis and campaign work runs every month.

How do you know the campaigns worked?+

A random group of customers receives no campaign. Comparing that holdout group with the contacted group estimates the work the campaign actually added, so results are measured rather than guessed.

Who owns the customer data?+

You do. The database belongs to your company. Ask for a full copy any time, and if we stop working together you keep it and everything in it.

Who runs CTSG?+

Anthony Johnson, a data specialist in Sugar Land, Texas, with 7+ years in data analytics, reporting and SQL. I do the analysis, build the campaigns and explain the report myself. No account managers, no subcontractors.
